1. Aviation Sector Mein Asli Problems (Existing Flaws & Challenges)

India ka domestic aviation sector duniya ke fastest-growing markets mein se ek hai. Lekin ticket pricing aur inflation monitoring ke field mein purane tarike real consumer ground reality se bilkul kate hue the. Niche 6 core problems hain jo is platform ne target ki hain:

1. Unweighted Arithmetic Average ki Dhokhadhadi
Traditional spot checks simple arithmetic average use karte hain. Indore–Coimbatore (jahan din ke 150 log udte hain) par ₹2,000 ka surge aur mega corridor Delhi–Mumbai (jahan din ke 25,000+ log udte hain) par ₹2,000 ka surge unweighted average ko barabar move karta hai—jabki aam janta ka hazaron guna zyada paisa Delhi-Mumbai par kharch hota hai.
2. No Official Airfare Price Index
Jaise daal, tel, petrol ke liye MoSPI ka Consumer Price Index (CPI) hota hai, waise aviation sector mein pure airfare inflation ko track karne ke liye koi scientific, empirical sovereign index exist nahi karta tha. CPI data aaj bhi manual physical inquiry se collect hota hai.
3. Predatory Surge Gouging (Emergency Pricing)
Airlines medical emergency, festival season, holiday peaks ya train cancellations ke dauran last-minute (T+1) tickets ko 200% se 400% tak surge kar deti hain. Regulatory agencies ke paas is gouging ko real-time capture karne ka automated tool nahi tha.
4. UDAN RCS Subsidies ka Misuse (VGF Risk)
Government airlines ko taxpayer money se ₹1,000+ Crore ki Viability Gap Funding (VGF) subsidy deti hai taaki tier-2/tier-3 cities mein aam nagrik saste mein ud sake. Lekin airlines subsidized regional routes par statutory fare caps (₹2,250 – ₹5,800) se zyada charge karke subsidy bhi claim kar leti hain.
5. Aviation Duopoly & Monopoly Risk
IndiGo aur Air India group milkar 85%–90% domestic capacity control karte hain. High-density corridors par cartelization aur artificial capacity restriction pakadne ke liye Competition Commission of India (CCI) ke paas route-by-route live HHI analytics tool nahi tha.
6. Reactive vs Proactive Regulation
DGCA action tab leti thi jab public social media par gussa karti thi ya Parliament mein question raise hota tha. Koi automated, legally-admissible real-time surveillance system exist nahi karta tha.

2. Yeh Platform Kya Kaam Karta Hai? (Platform Architecture & Core Engine)

UDAN-STAT / APIx pure desh ke 80 high-density corridors (~92% domestic passenger capacity) ka real-time fare data automate karke compute karta hai:

Modified Laspeyres Sovereign Index: APIx_t = Σ [ w_r × ( P_{r,t} / P_{r,0} ) ] × 100
Where w_r = Route Passenger Traffic Weight (DGCA Form A/B), P_{r,0} = Base Fare (Sept 2022 = 100.0)
  • 5 Booking Horizons: Temporal dynamic pricing curve ko capture karne ke liye 5 lead windows track hoti hain: T+1 (Emergency/Spot), T+7 (Headline Benchmark), T+15, T+30, aur T+45 (Advance Booking).
  • Statistical IQR Filtration: [Q1 - 1.5·IQR, Q3 + 1.5·IQR] inner fences apply karke temporary flash pricing, promotional error fares, aur unrepresentative single-seat spikes ko trim karta hai.
  • Robust Representative Median: Filtered distribution ka median fare P_{r,t} nikalta hai taaki kisi ek airline ke artificial rate se index manipulate na ho sake.

4. Platform Ke Har Feature Ka In-Depth Breakdown

📍 Feature 1: National Live Dashboard & Survey of India Route Map (/dashboard)

80 domestic corridors ka interactive live map jo 100% Survey of India boundaries ke sath compliant hai. Corridors dynamic inflation status (Normal, Moderate Surge, Critical Surge) ke mutabiq color-code hote hain. User $T+1$ se lekar $T+45$ tak horizons toggle karke live aggregate index aur national headline inflation dekh sakta hai.

🛡️ Feature 2: UDAN RCS Statutory Fare Cap Auditor (/udan-rcs)

Geodesic Haversine formula se airports ke beech great-circle distance calculate karta hai aur Gazette rules ke mutabiq distance-tiered fare ceilings (₹2,250 se ₹5,800) enforce karta hai. Violation pakde jane par 1-Click DGCA Show-Cause Legal Notice generate karta hai citing Rule 135 of Aircraft Rules, 1937 aur Viability Gap Funding (VGF) clawbacks flag karta hai.

⚖️ Feature 3: Antitrust & Market Concentration (HHI Matrix) (/analysts)

Har corridor par carriers ka flight share measure karke Herfindahl-Hirschman Index compute karta hai: HHI = Σ (s_i × 100)². Corridors ko Competitive (<1500), Moderate (1500–2500), aur High Concentration / Monopoly Risk (>2500) mein classify karta hai taaki CCI duopoly abuse ko rokk sake.

🚨 Feature 4: Statistical Outlier & Surge Gouging Auditor (/analysts)

IQR boundary analysis se live fare surge deviations track karta hai. Analysts slider se threshold (+20%, +50%, +80%) customize karke affected routes ko inspect kar sakte hain. System se 4 direct CSV datasets (fares, weights, anomalies, competition) export kiye ja sakte hain.

⛽ Feature 5: ATF (Jet Fuel) Price Shock Elasticity Simulator (/simulation)

Aviation Turbine Fuel (ATF) airlines ke OPEX ka ~35%–40% hota hai. Yeh simulator crude oil price shocks (-20% to +60%) aur configurable airline pass-through rates (η = 0.55 to 0.85) ke aadhar par calculate karta hai ki Indian air travelers par kitne ₹ Hazaar Crore ka extra consumer financial burden aayega.

🌐 Feature 6: Sovereign Gov API Gateway (/gov-api & /gov/v1/*)

NSO, RBI, DGCA, MoCA, aur CCI ke liye machine-readable REST endpoints jo X-Gov-API-Key se authenticated hain. Har response standard sovereign metadata envelope mein wrap hota hai jisme provenance, versioning, aur timestamp shamil hain.

📐 Feature 7: Econometric Methodology & Weight Allocation Portal (/methodology & /weights)

Pura mathematical formulation transparent hai—Laspeyres formula, IQR upper/lower fences, DGCA quarterly passenger traffic allocation matrix (w_r), aur base fares table freely accessible aur auditable hain.

🛩️ Feature 8: Fleet & Carrier Intelligence Radar (/fleet)

IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, Akasa Air aur AI Express ka 5-axis competitive radar (Pricing Aggressiveness, Fleet Age, OTP Punctuality, Surge Frequency, Network Coverage) aur smart consumer booking strategies provide karta hai.
